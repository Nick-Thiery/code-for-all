You've built your site twice: once from your first prompt, and once from the refined prompt. Time to look at what you made.

## Your gallery

1. Put your first draft and your refined version side by side.
2. Write down three things that changed.
3. Show both to someone.

Which of the changes came from something you added to the prompt? That's how you learn what a better prompt does.

<Callout kind="headsup">
If you share a link to your site, remember it's public. Check it has no full name, school, address, phone number or photos of you on it.
</Callout>

<Challenge title="Build a site that predicts something">
Build a Lovable site that predicts or forecasts something. A stock direction tool, a weather predictor, etc. Pick something real. Pull data from somewhere if you can. Show your reasoning for the prediction logic.

In plain words: make a site that guesses what will happen next, and explain how it guesses.

You can use tools like Claude to refine your prompt, as you did in [Using AI to make better prompts](/module-2/better-prompts-with-ai).

Building it needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).
</Challenge>
