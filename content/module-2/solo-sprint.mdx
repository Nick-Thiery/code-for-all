Time to build a first draft of the site you picked in [Today's task](/module-2/todays-task): a personality quiz, a news website or an online store.

<HandsOn />

## Build your first draft

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

1. **Draft a comprehensive descriptive prompt defining your target web portal layout.** In plain words: write a detailed prompt that describes your site and how it's laid out. Use the parts of the [strong prompt](/module-1/the-art-of-prompting) from Module 1, like who it's for, which pages it needs and how it should look.
2. **Paste the engineered instruction text into Lovable to create your website.** That's your prompt: paste it in and let Lovable build.
3. **Save your exact prompt.** This step matters most:

<Callout kind="headsup">
**Crucial:** Copy-paste and save your exact prompt text into a safe notes file for later edits.
</Callout>

You'll need that exact prompt in the next lesson, when you ask Claude to help you improve it.

## Look at your first draft

It's a first draft, so it doesn't have to be right. What did Lovable get right? What did it have to guess? The next two lessons are about making your prompt better, so the next version guesses less.
