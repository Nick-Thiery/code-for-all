## Check your refined prompt

- **Be specific about layout, colours, and audience.** If the refined prompt still leaves any of these vague, change it to say exactly what you mean.
- **Check over the prompt to ensure it has the elements you want.** It's your site. If something you want is missing, add it. If something's there that you don't want, take it out.

### How to check it

Read the refined prompt slowly, one line at a time. Then go through this list. Change the prompt in your notes file as you go.

- **Layout.** Does it name every page, and say what goes on each one?
- **Colours.** Does it name real colours or style words? "Cream background with one orange accent" tells Lovable much more than "a colourful design".
- **Audience.** Does it say who the site is for?
- **What you want.** Is everything you wanted in there? Look back at the two or three things you jotted down after your first draft.
- **What you don't want.** Take out anything you didn't ask for. Take out any login, sign in, database or real payments too: Lovable's own advice is to leave those out at first. ([Lovable's Getting started guide](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/getting-started))
- **Personal details.** No full names, school, address, phone number or photos of you.

### An example

Say Claude's refined prompt for the noodle quiz says:

> Use a colourful, modern design.

That's vague. Lovable will have to guess which colours, and "modern" could mean almost anything. You change it to:

> Design style: playful and bright. Cream background, one orange accent colour, big rounded buttons and friendly headings.

Now Lovable doesn't have to guess.

When you've finished checking, paste the final version into your notes file. If you changed it, update the saved box at the top of this lesson too.

## Paste it into Lovable

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

Paste it into Lovable and add the following before the refined prompt:

```prompt title="Add this before the refined prompt"
This is a refined prompt, make the required changes: 
```

So your message to Lovable is that line, followed straight away by your refined prompt.

### Pick one of two ways

You can use your refined prompt in two ways. Both work. Not sure? Use way 1.

- **Way 1: A fresh project.** Paste the refined prompt on its own into a new project. Your first draft stays exactly as it was, as its own project. To compare, open both from **Recents**.
- **Way 2: Change your first draft.** Paste the line above, then the refined prompt, into the chat of your first draft. Lovable keeps your first draft in **History**, so you can still look at it.

Way 2 is what the class did. It sends lots of changes in one message. That works, but Lovable's own advice is "one change per prompt", because a big prompt can make changes you didn't ask for. If that happens, way 2 shows you what to do. ([Lovable's guide, From idea to app](https://docs.lovable.dev/tips-tricks/from-idea-to-app))

Either way, a full rebuild uses more <Term def="Lovable's allowance for building. Each change you ask for uses some.">credits</Term> than a small change. On the Free plan you get 5 a day. ([Lovable's guide to credits](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage))

### Way 1: A fresh project

A new project builds your site from scratch. There's nothing to change yet, so you don't need the line in front.

1. Copy your refined prompt. Use the **Copy** button on it at the top of this lesson, or copy it from your notes file.
2. Open [Lovable](https://lovable.dev). You should see the dashboard, with the box that says "Ask Lovable to create…". If you see a project instead, click the menu icon at the top left to get back to the dashboard.
3. Check that the mode picker beside the send button says **Build**. If it says Chat or Plan, click it and choose **Build**.
4. Paste your refined prompt into the box and click the send button.
5. If Lovable shows design directions or asks about fonts and colours, pick the one closest to your refined prompt and click **Submit**.
6. Wait a few minutes while it builds. You should now see the editor, with your new version in the preview.
7. Give it a name you'll recognise, like "Noodle quiz, refined". On the dashboard, click **⋯** on its card, then **Edit**, then **Name**. ([Lovable's guide to the dashboard](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/dashboard-overview))
8. **Check it worked.** In the dashboard sidebar, click **Recents**. You should see two projects: your first draft and your refined version.

### Way 2: Change your first draft

1. Open your first draft. On the dashboard, click **Recents** in the sidebar, then click its card.
2. Check that the mode picker beside the chat box says **Build**.
3. Copy the line from the box above (use its **Copy** button) and paste it into the chat box at the bottom of the chat.
4. Copy your refined prompt and paste it straight after the line. Then press Enter.

   You should now see Lovable working on the changes. When it's done, the preview shows the new version.

5. Look at what changed. Did it change anything you didn't ask for?

   - **All good?** You're done.
   - **Something went wrong?** Click **Undo latest edit** under Lovable's latest reply to get your first draft back. ([Lovable's guide to version history](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/history)) Then send the changes one at a time instead, as Lovable recommends. Go through your refined prompt and send each difference as its own short message. Check the preview after each one before you send the next.

   Here are some example one-change messages:

   ```prompt title="Example: one change to the look"
   Make the background cream. Leave the rest of the site unchanged.
   ```

   ```prompt title="Example: one change to a page"
   Only change the results page: add an emoji and a short, funny description for each result. Leave the rest of the site unchanged.
   ```

6. **Check it worked.** The preview should show your refined version. Click **History** next to the project name: your first draft should be in the list.

## Compare with History

Lovable saves every change as a version, so your first draft is never lost. Here's how to look at it again. ([Lovable's guide to version history](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/projects/history))

1. Click **History** next to the project name.

   You should now see a list of versions, with two tabs: **History** and **Bookmarks**.

   <Screenshot caption="Lovable's History panel, with the History and Bookmarks tabs and a list of versions.">Lovable's History panel showing a list of versions with the History and Bookmarks tabs, from a neutral account with no names or avatars</Screenshot>

2. Click an older version, like your first draft. The preview shows that version. Nothing has changed yet: you're only looking.
3. To see it full screen in its own tab, open the version's **More actions** menu and choose **Open preview in new tab**. Keep that tab open for the [next lesson](/module-2/gallery).
4. Click **Back to latest** to return to your newest version.

Don't click **Revert** unless you want to go back to that version for real. Going back doesn't give back the credits a change used.

**Check it worked:** you can switch between your first draft and your refined version, either in **History** or between your two projects in **Recents**.
