## Check your refined prompt

- **Be specific about layout, colours, and audience.** If the refined prompt still leaves any of these vague, change it to say exactly what you mean.
- **Check over the prompt to ensure it has the elements you want.** It's your site. If something you want is missing, add it. If something's there that you don't want, take it out.

<HandsOn />

## Paste it into Lovable

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

Paste it into Lovable and add the following before the refined prompt:

```prompt title="Add this before the refined prompt"
This is a refined prompt, make the required changes: 
```

So your message to Lovable is that line, followed straight away by your refined prompt.

<Callout kind="tip">
Before you paste, take a screenshot of your first draft. In the [next lesson](/module-2/gallery) you'll put it side by side with the new version.
</Callout>

When Lovable is done, look at what changed.
