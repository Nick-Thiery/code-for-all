<KeyTerm term="Vibe coding">
Building software by telling an AI what you want, in plain words. You decide what it should do; the AI writes the code.
</KeyTerm>

The skill in vibe coding is asking well. That's what most of this module is about.

## Vibe coding in three steps

1. **Describe.** Convert your product requirements into clear, conversational <Term def="the messages you type to tell an AI what you want.">prompts</Term>. You define the rules; AI defines the architecture.
2. **Build.** AI will interpret requirements to generate responsive code, manage standard databases, and layout elements instantly.
3. **Launch.** Review the prototype, dynamically tweak specific features, sync with modern production tools, and push to cloud hosting under 5 seconds.

## What can you build?

Apps, Chrome extensions, websites, trading algorithms, and more.

For example, [Snapiq](https://snapiq.tools/) is a Chrome extension for taking screenshots, built by one of this course's tutors.

## Getting users

A product needs people to use it. Some ways to find them are SEO (search engine optimisation), Google Ads, social media marketing and word of mouth.

For example, one Google Ads campaign for the Snapiq website got about 16,200 clicks for $134.
