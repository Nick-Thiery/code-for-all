Bias means being unfair to some people without meaning to. AI can be biased too. Here are three things to keep in mind.

## Input bias

When entering a <Term>prompt</Term>, make sure you don't make it reflect your own bias.

An AI takes your words at face value. If your prompt assumes something about people, the result can too. For example, a prompt that says "for boys who like games" leaves out everyone else.

## Representative

If you are creating a tool such as ChatGPT, for example, you want it to give responses that represent a wide range of human perspectives.

In plain words: a tool that many people use should work well for all of them, not just for one kind of person.

## The amplification loop

When people use biased AI tools again and again, research suggests they can pick up the tool's biases over time.

So bias doesn't stay inside the tool. It can spread to the people who use it. That's why the first two points matter.
