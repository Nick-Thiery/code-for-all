## Input bias

When entering a prompt, make sure you don't make it reflect your own bias.

An AI takes your words at face value. If your prompt assumes something about people, the result will too.

## Representative

If you are creating a tool such as ChatGPT, for example, you want it to give responses that represent a wide range of human perspectives.

## The amplification loop

When people use biased AI tools again and again, research suggests they can pick up the tool's biases over time.

So bias doesn't stay inside the tool. It can spread to the people who use it, which is why the first two points matter.
