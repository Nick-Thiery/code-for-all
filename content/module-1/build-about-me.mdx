Time to build. You'll use the About Me prompt you wrote in [lesson 4](/module-1/the-art-of-prompting).

<Callout kind="headsup">
This site is about you, so keep it safe. Leave out your address, phone number, school and anything else you wouldn't tell a stranger. Your hobbies and the things you've made are plenty.
</Callout>

<HandsOn />

## Build it

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

1. Open Lovable.
2. Paste the About Me prompt you wrote in lesson 4.
3. Look at the result. What did Lovable get right? What did it have to guess?

## Change one part and compare

1. Pick one part of your prompt, like the design style or the target audience, and change it.
2. Run the new prompt in Lovable.
3. Compare the two results. What changed? Did it change the way you expected?
