Time to build. You'll use the About me prompt you wrote in [lesson 4](/module-1/the-art-of-prompting).

You'll make two versions of your site: one from your prompt, and one from the same prompt with one part changed. Then you'll compare them.

## Build it

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

1. Read your prompt once more. Take out anything the safety box above says to leave out, like your full name or your school.
2. Copy your prompt. Use the Copy button above.
3. Open [Lovable](https://lovable.dev).

   You should now see the dashboard, with the box that says "Ask Lovable to create…". If you see a project instead, click the menu icon at the top left to get back to the dashboard.

4. Check that the mode picker beside the send button says **Build**. If it says Chat or Plan, click it and choose **Build**.
5. Click in the box and paste your prompt: Ctrl+V on Windows or a Chromebook, Cmd+V on a Mac. Click the send button.
6. If Lovable shows three design directions or asks about fonts and colours, pick the one closest to your design style and click **Submit**.

   Your prompt already describes the look, so Lovable may skip this and start building straight away. Both are fine.

7. Wait while it builds. This takes a few minutes.

   You should now see the editor: the chat on the left and your About me site on the right.

8. Click **Mobile view** above the preview to see it at phone size, then **Desktop view** to go back.
9. **Check it worked.** You should see a website in the preview that's about you. Now look at the result. What did Lovable get right? What did it have to guess?

## Change one part and compare

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

1. Pick one part of your prompt, like the design style or the target audience, and change it.

   Change only that one line, so you know what caused any difference. Here are some examples of a changed line. Each one replaces just that line in your prompt:

   ```prompt title="Example: a new design style"
   Design style: calm and minimal, white background, thin dark headings, lots of space.
   ```

   ```prompt title="Example: a new target audience"
   Target audience: my grandparents, who don't use the internet much.
   ```

   ```prompt title="Example: new core pages"
   Core pages: a home section with a short hello, a Drawings gallery with six pictures, and a Games section with a card for each game I've made.
   ```

2. Run the new prompt in Lovable. Start a **new project** for it from the dashboard. Don't paste it into the chat of your first project.

   A new project keeps your first version safe, so you can compare the two side by side. Pasting a whole new prompt into the same chat would change your first site instead.

   To start a new project: go back to the dashboard, check the mode says **Build**, paste your changed prompt and click send. Pick a design direction if Lovable asks.

   Each new build uses credits, and a whole site uses more than a small change. On Lovable's Free plan you get 5 a day. If you run out before the second build, your credits come back at 8am Singapore time the next day. ([Lovable's guide to credits](https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/credits-and-usage))

3. Compare the two results. What changed? Did it change the way you expected?

   To switch between them, open each one from **Recents** in the dashboard sidebar. Look at the colours, the fonts, the words and the sections. Check both in **Mobile view** too.

4. **Check it worked.** You should have two projects in **Recents**: your first build and the changed one. You should be able to say which part you changed and one thing that changed because of it.

## Make small fixes

Want to fix one thing on a site, like a heading? Don't rewrite the whole prompt. Lovable's advice is "one change per prompt, verify in the preview, then move on". ([Lovable's guide, From idea to app](https://docs.lovable.dev/tips-tricks/from-idea-to-app))

- Type one change in the chat box, like "Make the Projects heading bigger", and press Enter.
- Say what must stay the same: "Only change the Projects section. Leave the rest of the site unchanged."
- To change a few words, use **Edit text inline** in the preview toolbar instead. A limited number are free each day.
- Didn't like the change? Click **Undo latest edit**.

## Put it online (optional)

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

Want to share your site? Check it against the safety box at the top of this lesson first. On the Free plan, anyone with the link can visit, and you can't make it private. ([Lovable's guide to publishing](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/publish))

1. Open the project you want to share. Click **Publish** at the top right of the editor, next to **Share**. On a narrow window it may show only an icon.

   You should now see a box with a **Website URL** ending in `lovable.app`. You can change the first part of the address before you publish. Lovable runs a quick security check, which takes about 10 seconds.

2. Click **Publish**.
3. **Check it worked.** You should see **Your website is live**, with buttons to copy the link and visit the site. Click to visit it and check it looks right.

Publishing this way doesn't use credits. Sites on the Free plan show a small **Edit with Lovable** badge.

The live site is a snapshot. If you change your project later, the live site doesn't change until you click **Publish**, then **Publish changes**. A small dot on **Publish** means you have changes that aren't live yet. To take the site down, open the Publish box, click the **⋮** menu and choose **Unpublish**.
