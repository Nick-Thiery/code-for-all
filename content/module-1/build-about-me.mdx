## Build it

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

1. Open Lovable.
2. Paste your About Me prompt. Use the Copy button above.
3. Look at the result. What did Lovable get right? What did it have to guess?

Here's the kind of site a short prompt gets you. Kai is made up, so nothing here is about a real person.

<Figure src="/lessons/module-1/about-me-first-draft.png" alt="An example About Me website built from a short prompt. A dark header says About Me. Below it are a grey box marked Photo, a Welcome heading with the line 'Hi, my name is Kai. Welcome to my website. This is a website about me.', an About section saying 'I am a student. I like sports, music and computers. I am learning to build websites.', and an Interests list: Sports, Music, Computers." width={1500} height={960} caption="An example About Me site from a short prompt. Look at how much it had to guess: which sections to make, how it should look, even what to say. Everything your prompt leaves out, Lovable fills in with something generic.

<HandsOn />

## Change one part and compare

1. Pick one part of your prompt, like the design style or the target audience, and change it.
2. Run the new prompt in Lovable.
3. Compare the two results. What changed? Did it change the way you expected?
