The course has 10 modules in three phases: [Build with AI](/#module-1) (Modules 1 to 5), [Developer fundamentals](/#module-6) (Modules 6 to 8) and [Your final project](/#module-9) (Modules 9 and 10).

## Hands-on first

- Short lessons to learn the basics
- Hands-on activities to learn how to use tools
- Homework to apply your learning. Here, that's the **Challenge** at the end of each module.

It won't take long and it will be fun!

## Build to launch

- Learn how to make a product
- Use search engine optimisation (SEO)
- Market your product

## What you'll need

You can read every lesson on a phone. For the hands-on parts, you need a laptop.

Some lessons have a hands-on part that uses a tool like Lovable, which needs an account. Those lessons are marked **Hands-on: needs access**, and the reading part always works without it. [How hands-on access works](/access).

## Getting help

- **Stuck?** Hands-on lessons have a "Stuck?" box near the end, with common problems and how to fix them.
- **Join a Code for All session.** A teacher or tutor runs it, so there's someone to ask, and hands-on access is set up for you there. [How sessions and access work](/access).
