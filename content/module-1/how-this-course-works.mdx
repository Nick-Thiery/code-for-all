The course has 10 modules in three phases: [Build with AI](/#module-1) (Modules 1 to 5), [Developer fundamentals](/#module-6) (Modules 6 to 8) and [Your final project](/#module-9) (Modules 9 and 10).

A module is a group of short lessons on one topic. You can do them at your own pace.

## Hands-on first

- Short lessons to learn the basics
- Hands-on activities to learn how to use tools
- Homework to apply your learning. Here, that's the **Challenge** at the end of each module.

It won't take long and it will be fun!

## Build to launch

- Learn how to make a product
- Use search engine optimisation (SEO)
- Market your product

In plain words: you'll make something real, help people find it, and tell people about it.

## What you'll need

You can read every lesson on a phone. Practice and quizzes work on a phone too.

For the hands-on parts, you need a laptop or a Chromebook. Not sure what your device can do? See [Which device do you have?](/access#devices)

Some lessons have a hands-on part that uses a tool like Lovable. Those lessons are marked **Hands-on: needs access**. They start with a "You'll need" box that says what the lesson uses. The reading part always works without it. [How hands-on access works](/access).

Everything in this course is free. Nothing will ever ask you for a bank card.

## Getting help

- **Stuck?** Hands-on lessons have a "Stuck?" box near the end, with common problems and how to fix them.
- **Help page.** The [Help page](/help) answers the big questions: no laptop, a school device that blocks installs, lost progress, and what to do when something breaks.
- **Join a Code for All session.** A teacher or tutor runs it, so there's someone to ask. Hands-on access is set up for you there. [How sessions and access work](/access).
