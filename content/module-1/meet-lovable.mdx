Lovable is a vibe coding tool. You describe what you want, and it builds it: the pages, the code and the layout. You'll use it for the rest of this module and in Module 2.

<HandsOn />

## Open Lovable and experiment

Open [Lovable](https://lovable.dev). This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

<Figure src="/lessons/module-1/lovable-projects.png" alt="Lovable's project list, with tabs for Recently viewed, My projects and Templates, and preview cards of three websites made in Lovable, two of them marked Published." width={1510} height={384} caption="Projects in Lovable, with tabs for recently viewed projects, your own projects and templates." />

<Callout kind="tryit">
Open Lovable and try any one-line idea. Type what you want to build in a single sentence and see what it makes.
</Callout>

It doesn't have to be good. This is about seeing what Lovable does with a short prompt. In [lesson 4](/module-1/the-art-of-prompting) you'll see why a longer one works better.
