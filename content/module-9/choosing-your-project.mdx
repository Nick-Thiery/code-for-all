## Two kinds of project

<ProjectPathsDiagram />

- **Business-forward product.** A Chrome extension, an automation, a <Term def="software as a service: an app people use online, often for a subscription.">SaaS</Term>, or a service.
- **Social impact product.** Something that helps businesses, or raises awareness of a problem.

Both are real projects. Pick the one you care about more.

**Challenge yourself. Don't pick low hanging fruit.**

Low hanging fruit means the easy option. An easy project teaches you less, and it's less interesting to show.

## Alone or together

The original class split into groups of 3. Here, work alone or with friends. If you work with friends, use branches and pull requests the way you did in [Module 7](/module-7).

<Callout kind="tip">
Some projects grow into real businesses. That's optional, not the point. A project you're proud of and that someone actually uses is the goal.
</Callout>
