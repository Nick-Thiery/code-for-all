A product needs people to use it. In [Module 1](/module-1/what-is-vibe-coding) you saw one example: a Google Ads campaign for the Snapiq website. Here are the channels the course covers.

<GettingUsersDiagram />

## Google Ads

Paid adverts on Google. Your site appears at the top when someone searches for what you do, and you pay for each click.

## Email automation

Emails that send themselves. Someone signs up, and your site sends them a welcome email, then a reminder, without you doing anything.

## Affiliate marketing

Other people promote your product and get a share of what they bring in.

## Social media marketing

Posts about your product on social media, so people find it where they already are.

## SMMAs

A social media marketing agency: a company that runs social media for other businesses.

<Callout kind="headsup">
Adverts and agencies cost money. Nothing in this course asks you to pay for anything. For your project, start with the free channels: search, your own posts, and telling people. In [Module 10](/module-10) you'll show your project to someone, which is word of mouth.
</Callout>
