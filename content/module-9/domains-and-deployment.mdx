A <Term def="a web address you own, like example.com. It's a name in the DNS phonebook.">domain</Term> is the name people type to reach your site.

## Buying a domain

You can buy a domain through Lovable, or through a <Term def="a company that sells domain names, like GoDaddy.">registrar</Term> like GoDaddy.

Which ending? **.com** usually works. **.org** or **.edu** are for other reasons: .org is often used by non-profit groups, and .edu by schools and universities.

<Callout kind="headsup">
You don't need to buy a domain for this course. A free Vercel address, like your-project.vercel.app, is fine. Buying a domain costs money and needs a card, so ask a parent or guardian first, and never enter card details yourself.
</Callout>

## Deploying your project

Deploying means putting your project online. There are two routes:

1. **Lovable's method.** If you built in Lovable, publish from Lovable and connect your domain there.
2. **The standard route.** Push your code to GitHub and let Vercel build it, the way you did in [Module 6](/module-6/connect-to-vercel).

<CodeJourneyDiagram />

Either way, every change you make can go live in minutes.
