You built it. Now people need to find it. The first way is through search.

<KeyTerm term="SEO">
Search engine optimisation: improving a site to rank in unpaid search results.
</KeyTerm>

"Rank" means where your site comes in the list when someone searches. Higher is better. "Unpaid" means you didn't buy an advert to get there.

## The four key components

<SeoDiagram />

1. **On-page.** Keywords in titles, meta descriptions, headings and content. A <Term def="a word or phrase people type into a search engine.">keyword</Term> is what people search for. A <Term def="the short description of a page that search engines show under its title.">meta description</Term> is the short summary a search engine shows under your page's title.
2. **Off-page.** Authority and <Term def="links from other websites to yours. Search engines treat them as votes.">backlinks</Term>. When other sites link to yours, search engines trust you more.
3. **Technical.** Crawlability, speed and mobile-friendliness. Crawlability means search engines can read every page of your site.
4. **Content quality.** Genuinely useful content. This is the one that matters most: there's no trick that beats being useful.

</Callout>
