## Deploy it

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

Use the route from [Module 6](/module-6/connect-to-vercel): push your project to GitHub and let Vercel build it. Built it in Lovable? Publish from Lovable instead. Either way, you get a public link.

## Check it ships

This is the first of the five things a finished project needs. Open your live link on a phone and on a laptop, and tick each one:

- Is your project live at a working URL that anyone can open?
- Does it load properly on a phone and on a laptop?
- Do all your pages and links work, with none broken?

Try it as a stranger would: open the link in a private window, where you're not logged in to anything.
