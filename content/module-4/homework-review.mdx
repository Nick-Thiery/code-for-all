The challenge at the end of [Module 3](/module-3) was to build a Chrome extension with Claude Code. Before you learn something new, look back at what you made.

## Self-review

Open your extension and answer these four questions. Write your answers in your notes.

- Does it install cleanly and run?
- What problem does it solve?
- What was the hardest part to get working?
- If it worked perfectly, what exactly should it do?

Not working yet? Go back to the Module 3 challenge and keep going. Your answer to the last question is exactly what to tell Claude Code.
