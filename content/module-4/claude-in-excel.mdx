Claude doesn't only work in a chat window. It can also work inside Microsoft Excel, right next to your spreadsheet.

## Advanced Data Operations

Use Claude directly inside Excel to analyze, explain, and act on your spreadsheet data — no switching tabs or copy-pasting.

- **Formula Assistance:** Write, fix, and explain complex <Term def="a calculation in a spreadsheet cell, such as adding up a column.">formulas</Term> in plain English
- **Data Analysis:** Summarize trends, flag <Term def="numbers that don't fit the pattern of the rest.">anomalies</Term>, and interpret results across large datasets
- **Audit & Validation:** Trace logic errors and verify <Term def="the rules a business's numbers are meant to follow.">business rules</Term> step-by-step

## What it looks like

<Figure src="/lessons/module-4/claude-in-excel-question.jpg" alt="An Excel spreadsheet holding a financial model for Molson Coors Brewing Company, with rows of cash flow figures for FY2020 to FY2025. The Claude panel on the right shows the question: hello claude, analyze this sheet and tell me the 5 year cagr net debt change. Claude replies that it will find the net debt line in the Model sheet and compute the 5-year CAGR, and is still responding." width={1067} height={556} caption="Claude in Excel. Claude opens in a panel next to the sheet." />

In this example, someone asked Claude about a company's financial spreadsheet:

```prompt title="The question in the screenshot"
hello claude, analyze this sheet and tell me the 5 year cagr net debt change
```

In plain English: on average, how much did the company's debt change each year over five years?

<Figure src="/lessons/module-4/claude-in-excel-answer.jpg" alt="The same spreadsheet, with Claude's answer in the side panel. The 5-year CAGR of net debt, FY2020 to FY2025, is minus 6.3%. It lists FY2020 net debt of $7,458.2M and FY2025 net debt of $5,403.0M, each with a blue label naming the cell it came from (Model AT638 and Model BS638), shows the calculation, says net debt has fallen by roughly 6.3% a year over the five years, and asks whether to put the calculation into a cell on the Model or Summary Page." width={973} height={526} caption="Claude finds the numbers it needs and works out the answer. The blue labels show which cells they came from." />

Claude found the two numbers it needed, showed which cells they came from, worked out the answer, and explained what it means for the company.
