In this module you'll design a website in Claude Design, hand it to Claude Code to build, then launch it. You'll see the whole workflow in [lesson 7](/module-5/the-whole-workflow). It starts with the design.

Claude Design runs in your browser, through your Code for All access. Nothing to install, nothing to pay.

## What Claude Design does

- Claude Design allows you to build the visual shell of your site.
- Describe how you want it to look without any code!

The <Term def="the part of a website people see and use: the words, the layout, the colours and the buttons.">front end</Term> is the part people see. A visual shell is the look of the site before it does anything. Making it actually work comes later, when you hand your design to Claude Code.
