You met Claude Code in [Module 3](/module-3). Here's what it does with your design once you hand it off.

## An agent, not a chatbot

Claude Code is an **agent**, not a chatbot. Instead of just replying in chat, it works directly on the files on your computer.

A chatbot tells you what to change. An agent makes the change.

## It splits your design into files

It splits your single design into proper, separate files: `index.html`, `styles.css` and `script.js`. You'll see what each one is for in [lesson 6](/module-5/the-three-files).

## It works on its own

It works on its own: running commands, reading files, and deciding what to do next without you guiding each step.

## Errors are normal

Errors are normal and not a sign you broke something. If you hit an API limit error, just type `continue` and it picks up exactly where it left off.
