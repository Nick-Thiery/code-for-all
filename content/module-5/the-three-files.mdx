When Claude Code builds your site, it splits it into three files: `index.html`, `styles.css` and `script.js`. Each one has its own job. Know which is which, and you'll know where to look when you want to change something.

<ThreeFilesDiagram />

## index.html

The words and the structure: every heading, button and paragraph people actually read.

## styles.css

The look: colours, fonts, spacing, and how it changes on a phone versus a laptop.

## script.js

The behaviour: what happens when somebody clicks, types, or hits submit.
