Your <Term>branch</Term> is on GitHub. Now it needs to join <Term>main</Term>. You met <Term term="pull request">pull requests</Term> and merging in [Module 6's key terms](/module-6/key-terms). Here's how they work with a branch.

<HandsOn />

## Open, review, merge

This part needs access; see [how hands-on access works](/access).

1. **Open PR.** After your push, a yellow **Compare & pull request** banner appears on the repo page. Click it, write one line about your change, then click **Create pull request**.
2. **Teammate reviews.** A teammate opens the **Files changed** tab: green lines were added, red lines removed. They comment, suggest edits, or say it looks good.
3. **Merge it.** Your teammate clicks **Merge pull request** → **Confirm merge**. Your branch joins main, and Vercel redeploys the live site automatically.

<Callout kind="headsup">
It's that easy. But remember, step two can only be done by you, not Claude. However, you can ask Claude to help you review the changes that your teammates made.

Step two here means this whole lesson: opening, reviewing and merging the pull request. It's not just item 2 in the list above.
</Callout>

Working on your own? In the [task](/module-7/recreate-wordle) you'll be your own team, so you'll review and merge your own pull requests.
</Callout>
