Build it

Four steps, about ten minutes.
  1. 1Open LovableOpen it in a new tab, so this lesson stays open too.
  2. 2Paste your promptPaste it into the box and press Enter.
  3. 3Look at the resultWhat did Lovable get right? What did it have to guess?
  4. 4Change one partChange the style or the pages in your prompt. Run it again and compare the two.
“Lovable guesses whatever you leave out.”

Keep it safe

 This site is about you, so use your first name only. Leave out your school, address, phone number and photos of yourself.
Why first name only?

Anything you publish can be seen by anyone. A first name is friendly, but it doesn't tell strangers who or where you are.
