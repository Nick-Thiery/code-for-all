Getting a Claude account

Two lessons have a hands-on part that needs a Claude account. Everything else on Code for All works without one.

Works without an account

  • Reading every lesson, including the hands-on ones
  • Practice with AI feedback
  • Saving your progress on this device

What's a Claude account?

Claude is an AI made by a company called Anthropic. An account lets you use Claude Code, the AI agent you'll build your website with.

Why do some lessons need one?

In Lessons 5 and 7, Claude Code runs on your own laptop and does real work for you. It needs an account behind it to do that.

How do I get one?

Accounts come through a Code for All session, or through a teacher or group that runs one. They set it up for you, so you don't need to sign up for anything on your own.

No account yet?

That's okay. Read the hands-on parts anyway so you know every step. When you get access, you'll be ready to go.
