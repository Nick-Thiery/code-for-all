Navigation model

One change to your URLs: lessons sit under their Part (/part-1/how-a-language-model-works), and so does the complete page (/part-1/complete). Every URL shows where you are, slugs only need to be unique within a Part, and Part 2+ slot in with no restructuring. /lessons/<slug> can redirect.

Header: logo and Course both go home, where the track lives. Mobile keeps both links visible: only the logo shrinks to its hexagon.

In a lesson: the header adds “Lesson 2 of 7” with seven hex pips. It's inline on desktop and a slim second row on mobile. The Part name at the top of each lesson links back to the track.

Between lessons: prev/next at the end, with next filled and larger. The last lesson's next goes to Part complete.

One click to continue: on home, the hero button becomes “Continue: <lesson>”. On every other non-lesson page, returning learners get a slim resume bar under the header. Progress lives in localStorage, and there are no accounts.

Nothing is locked inside a released Part. Only unreleased Parts are.
